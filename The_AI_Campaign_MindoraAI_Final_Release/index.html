About The AI Campaign

The AI Campaign is a standalone Mindora AI learning programme focused on the responsible use of artificial intelligence and modern communication practices in campaign environments.

Learning approach

Short lessons, applied exercises, reflection prompts, practical templates and a capstone portfolio. The course emphasises transferable skills rather than partisan persuasion tactics.

Participation and recognition

There is no pass-mark exam. Participants who take part in and complete the programme activities may receive a Certificate of Participation. A certificate recognises participation; it is not a claim of government accreditation or professional licensure.
